How Much Does Rocket Languages Really Cost in 2026?

6 min read · Updated September 2026

Rocket Languages doesn't run a monthly subscription like most language apps — it sells one-time, lifetime access per level. That's good news if you hate recurring bills, but it also means the sticker price looks bigger upfront. Here's exactly what you pay, what you get, and where the real discounts are.

How the pricing actually works

Each language is split into three levels — Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced — and you can buy them separately or bundled. Whatever you buy, you keep forever: no expiry, no renewal, and free access to future updates for that course. Full retail price is listed at roughly $150 per level, but Rocket runs a discount essentially year-round, so almost nobody pays that list price.

What's actually included in the price

The full three-level bundle includes roughly 370 hours of lesson content, thousands of phrases with voice-recognition practice, dozens of interactive audio lessons, and a matching set of culture lessons for context. It also comes with a bonus "Survival Kit" of essential phrases per level, thrown in at no extra cost.

Free trial, payment plans and refunds

You can try roughly 10 lessons for free before paying anything. If the one-time cost is a stretch, Rocket offers a 6-month payment plan with no interest, and there's a family discount if more than one person in your household wants to learn. Every purchase is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, so you have two months to decide it isn't for you and get a full refund.

Is it worth the price?

Compared to a private tutor or an in-person class, a one-time payment in the $99–$259 range for lifetime access is inexpensive. Compared to a free app, it's clearly a bigger commitment — but you're paying for a structured course with real voice recognition, not just a habit-tracking game. If you're unsure, start with the free lesson and decide from there.
